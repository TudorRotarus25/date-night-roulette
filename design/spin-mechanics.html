What each one costs you

My read after building all three. The honest summary: the wheel is the most fun and the least legible, and the gap closes entirely once you stop trying to fit twenty names on it.

1 · The full wheel

Charming, cramped
  • Honest — you can see every option and that nothing's rigged
  • The single most satisfying spin of the three
  • 18° per segment. Names shrink to 9px and most get truncated
  • Gets worse every time you add a restaurant — degrades as the app succeeds
  • The emoji does all the work; the name is decoration

2 · The finalists

My recommendation
  • 51° per segment — names and emoji both properly readable
  • Two beats of drama instead of one: the shortlist, then the spin
  • Still perfectly uniform — a uniform draw of 7 then a uniform spin is uniform overall
  • Never degrades. Works identically at 20 restaurants or 200
  • One extra tap
  • Needs a sentence of explanation the first time

3 · The tumble

Safest, least special
  • Full name and emoji at readable size, always
  • Simplest to build — no SVG geometry, no angle maths
  • Completely indifferent to pool size
  • You can't see the field, so you take the result on trust
  • Reads as a utility. It picks for you; it isn't an occasion

The fairness question, settled

Mechanic 2 does not bias the outcome. Drawing 7 uniformly from 20, then spinning uniformly among those 7, gives every restaurant exactly 1-in-20. The shortlist is theatre, not a thumb on the scale — which matters, because you chose a uniform unfiltered wheel precisely so there'd be nothing to argue about.

The one thing it changes is visible rather than statistical: you see seven names instead of twenty, so you can't confirm by eye that a particular place was in the running tonight. If that bothers you, mechanic 1 is the only one that shows the whole field — and you'd be buying that reassurance with legibility.
